BLACK privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use BLACK at black.getservices.ai. It applies alongside the policies of GetServices.ai.

Short answer

Your briefs are sent to AI providers to produce answers. Attached spreadsheets are read in your browser and only a summary is sent. Payments are handled by Stripe. Calendar events you create are stored so BLACK Calendar can show them. BLACK does not sell your data.

Your briefs and answers

When you send a brief, BLACK's gateway passes it to one or more AI providers so they can answer: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT), Google (Gemini), Perplexity and xAI (Grok). Each provider handles that data under its own terms. BLACK's gateway does not save your briefs or answers to a database; it keeps a count of how many briefs you've sent this billing period to apply your plan's allowance.

Attached spreadsheets

Excel and CSV files are read inside your browser. Only a compact summary of the data is added to your brief and sent to the AI engines. The file itself is not uploaded to or stored on BLACK's servers.

Bank statements

Bank statement files (OFX, QFX, QBO, bank-style CSV or Excel exports and text PDFs) are also read inside your browser. Nothing is stored. Only a compact summary, with account numbers masked, is sent with your brief.

Websites in your brief

If your brief names a website, BLACK may open that page to read it so the answer reflects what is actually there.

Sign-in

BLACK uses your GetServices.ai account. Your email and password are passed securely from BLACK's server to the GetServices account service to sign you in.

Payments

Plans are paid through Stripe's secure checkout. BLACK and GetServices.ai never see or store your full card number. We keep your plan, its status and billing dates so BLACK knows which allowance applies, and Stripe keeps your invoices, which you can download in Manage billing.

Enterprise enquiries

If you contact us about Enterprise, we keep the name, email, company, team size and message you send and email them to the GetServices.ai team so we can reply.

BLACK Calendar

Events you create, scheduling proposals and any calendar you import are stored so BLACK Calendar can show them. A private calendar (iCal) address is stored encrypted. When you ask a calendar question, the next 14 days of your events are sent to the AI engines as context for that answer.

In your browser

BLACK stores small settings in your browser, such as your sign-in session, preview access and console preferences, so you do not have to set them each time. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Questions and requests

To ask about your data or request deletion of your calendar data, contact GetServices.ai. We will update this page as BLACK adds features.

Need help?
