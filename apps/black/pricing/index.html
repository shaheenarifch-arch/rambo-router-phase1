BLACK pricing and access

Last updated October 31, 2026

BLACK is the dark-and-gold executive edition of GetServices.ai. It is open to everyone: sign in, get 50 free credits, and choose a plan when you need more.

Short answer

Sign in with Google or your GetServices account to get 50 free credits. After that, BLACK Starter is $99/month and Pro is $299/month, both monthly with a 7-day free trial. Enterprise is by request.

How credits work

  • Fast or Smart brief: 1 credit
  • Deep brief: 2 credits
  • Executive brief (up to three engines plus a synthesis): 3 credits
  • Credits are used only when a brief is answered. Failed briefs are free.
  • Your balance is shown at the top of the console. Tap it to see what you have used and to choose a plan.

How to start

  1. Open the Unlock BLACK window.
  2. Choose Continue with Google, or sign in with your GetServices email and password. No account? Create one free.
  3. The console opens with your 50 free credits ready.

Online billing is being connected. Until then, choosing a plan in the console sends your request to the GetServices team, who activate it for you. Nobody is charged automatically.

Questions

Is BLACK free?

You can start free. Every new account gets 50 free credits after signing in with Google or a GetServices email. When they run out, choose a plan to keep going.

What does a credit cost?

A Fast or Smart brief uses 1 credit, Deep uses 2 and Executive uses 3. Credits are used only when a brief is answered; failed briefs are free.

Do I need a separate account?

No. BLACK uses your GetServices.ai account, the same login as ATLAS and AI Studio. Google sign-in works too.
