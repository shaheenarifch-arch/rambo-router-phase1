How to unlock BLACK

  1. Open the Unlock BLACK window.
  2. Choose Enter BLACK. Preview access is saved in that browser.
  3. In the console, tap the status badge and enter your access code to switch from Preview to Live answers.

Questions

Is BLACK free right now?

Yes. BLACK is in preview. Tap Unlock BLACK on the home page and choose Enter BLACK to open the console. Live answers from every engine need an access code, which you enter by tapping the status badge in the console.

Will BLACK be paid?

Yes. The unlock will become a paid upgrade. Pricing has not been announced yet; this page will be updated before billing starts, and nobody will be charged automatically when preview ends.

Do I need a separate account?

No. BLACK uses your GetServices.ai account and shares your login and credits with GetServices AI Studio.
