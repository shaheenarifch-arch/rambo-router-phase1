Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Your first BLACK plan starts with a 7-day free trial. A card is required at checkout, and you won't be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

What counts as a brief?

Each question you send to BLACK is one brief, counted by mode: Fast and Smart share one allowance, and Deep and Executive have their own. A brief that fails on every engine isn't counted.

What happens when I reach my allowance?

BLACK tells you and opens Plan & billing. You can move to Pro, use another mode, or wait for your allowance to reset at the start of your next billing period.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time in Manage billing in the console. Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Do I need a separate account?

No. BLACK uses your GetServices.ai account. If you don't have one, you can create it when you start your trial.
