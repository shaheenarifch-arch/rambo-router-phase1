What executives use BLACK for

Last updated October 30, 2026

BLACK is built for one person with too many decisions. Here is what it does well, with briefs you can paste straight into the console.

Short answer

Board-ready reports, portfolio reviews, live market and competitor research, analysis of your own spreadsheets, and scheduling through BLACK Calendar.

Board reports

Turn notes and numbers into a board-ready brief. Executive Mode asks several engines and flags where they disagree.

Try: Draft a one-page Q4 board update from these figures, with risks and the three decisions we need.

Strategic portfolio management

Review projects, budgets and dependencies, then rank what to fund, pause or stop.

Try: Rank these eight initiatives by value and risk and recommend which two to pause.

Markets and competitors

Perplexity and Grok bring live web results with sources, so answers reflect what is happening now.

Try: What changed in the US AI services market this week, and what does it mean for a small consultancy?

Your spreadsheets

Attach an Excel or CSV file and the Docs Analyst answers from your data, with a table and a chart.

Try: Which five customers grew most this year, and which are at risk? (with a sales export attached)

Meetings and calendar

Sign in with your GetServices account to use BLACK Calendar. Ask what is coming up, or ask BLACK to schedule, move or cancel a meeting. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Try: Schedule a 30-minute strategy review next Tuesday afternoon.

Questions

Is BLACK financial advice?

No. BLACK gives research and analysis to support your decisions. It is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Check important figures with a qualified professional.

Which mode should I use?

Use Smart for quick answers, Deep for a full report and Executive when the decision is important and you want several engines compared.
